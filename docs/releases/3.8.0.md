**Beluga 3.8.0: your agents' traces in your own tools, endpoints in OpenAI's format, schedules for everyone, and new worktrees you can write to right away.**

- **Send traces to your own tools**: your agents' traces go to Ratel Cloud, Braintrust, PostHog, LangSmith, Arize, Logfire, Sentry, New Relic, Datadog, Langfuse, Databricks or any OTLP endpoint, each with the attributes its own docs ask for.
- **Endpoints in OpenAI's format**: a local or bring-your-own endpoint can now speak OpenAI's `/v1/chat/completions` (Format: OpenAI). A translator on this computer turns it into what Claude Code expects, so vLLM servers and gateways without a working `/v1/messages` just work.
- **Schedules leave Experimental**: Schedule is a mode in the composer, and `/schedule` turns the box into a schedule. Plan with Tasks is now Advanced Planning, Full Autopilot is a switch in the mode menu, and Build With is Builder, shown only when there is someone to pick.
- **Write while the worktree is made**: a new worktree's chat takes your message straight away, and its agent gets it once the worktree is ready. Making the worktree shows as the agent's first step instead of a wait.
- **Sharing with friends**: one ladder for what a friend can do (see, chat, fork, fork with secrets). A fork brings the git-ignored context you pick, and secrets only when you tick them.
- **Mentions in friend chats**: @ in a DM or channel offers your projects, chats and worktrees, and dropping a worktree's or chat's row on a friend conversation puts its chip in your message.
- **Plans from a main folder**: a plan made in a main-folder chat shows the real plan, and Read in full appears only when the plan is longer than the card.
- **Fixes**: task calls without a key reach the task Claude just defined, or the one a plan opened; a merged task held for its criteria goes Done once its worktree is gone; your own first message in a new worktree isn't marked unread; the branch button's ▾ keeps its Merged/Conflicts colour when pressed; on Windows, panel tabs drag and dropped files keep their paths; Beluga's worktree git never lands in another repository when git was started from a hook.

The phone app is 3.8.0 too, with chat schedules for everyone, and it updates by itself.

The Android app (`Beluga-3.8.0.apk`) and Beluga Link (`Beluga-Link.apk`) are attached.
