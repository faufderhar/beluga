**Beluga 3.6.1: Full Autopilot, a planner, builder and checker for each plan, questions that wait for you, and Push main to origin.**

- **Full Autopilot**: turn it on for a chat (the composer's mode menu) or a plan (Autopilot › *Never wait for me*), and a stand-in agent answers whatever your own agents would ask you: questions, plans to approve, a plan's decisions and your own runs' approvals. A quiet line in the chat says what it chose and why. Secrets, friends' requests and tasks only you can do always stay yours.
- **Who plans, builds and checks**: a plan has a planner, a builder and a checker, each any agent and model. New Plan says it as one sentence, and Mix Agents gives each part its own picker.
- **Questions wait for you**: your own agent's questions and plans no longer get skipped after 5 minutes. Only work on Autopilot gives up, after the wait you pick in Settings › Agent › Autopilot (5 minutes to 4 hours, or until you answer).
- **Push main to origin**: after Straight to main, a worktree whose work is all on main shows how many commits aren't pushed, with Push main to origin as its main step. It fetches first and never forces.
- **Delete several at once**: in the Overview, ⌘-click, Shift-click or ⌘A to pick worktrees and chats, then delete them with one question.
- **Long pastes as a file**: a paste of 6,000+ characters or 120+ lines goes as a text file chip, and the agent gets its first lines and the file.
- **OpenCode, Codex and Copilot from npm**: Beluga finds them installed with npm under nvm, fnm, Volta or pnpm too.
- **Chinese**: 373 more Mac strings in 简体中文, and Enter that picks an input method's words never sends the message.
- **Computers on different versions**: a paired computer on an older or newer Beluga no longer crashes the window or shows as offline.
- **Onboarding**: a tour you can play with.
- **Fixes**: Archive Instead in the delete dialog archives again; a picture you send sits on the right; Copy Image works again; chats drag onto each other with sidebar categories on; the home box's Name and Then no longer overlap; git's messages are read right on a Mac in another language; the board reads the words agents explain a criterion with.

The phone app is 3.6.1 too, with Full Autopilot, a plan's three parts and Push main to origin, and it updates by itself.
